About Me

Jizdan YR is a student of Informatics Engineering at Politeknik Negeri Semarang. He builds across the full stack: web apps, desktop tools, and augmented reality experiences. His work ranges from real-time hand-tracking particle engines and AR character visualizers to finance trackers with 3D charts and cloud-synced productivity suites.
